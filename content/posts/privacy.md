This policy covers the internal tools that DRMSR Digital & Health Solutions (drmsr.dev) uses to work with Google services. It was last updated on 31 October 2026.

## What the tools access

DRMSR's own tools connect to one Google account, which belongs to Dr. Syamirul, the owner of DRMSR. They read and write that account's own Google Docs and Google Drive files, and they do this only to prepare his own documents, such as quotations and proposals.

## What is stored

The only thing kept is the sign-in token that Google issues to the tool. It is stored on DRMSR's own computer and is never published or shared. The contents of documents are not copied into any other database.

## Who it is shared with

Nobody. No data from Google is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or for training models.

## Who can use the tools

The tools are for DRMSR's internal use. They are not offered to the public, and they do not access any other person's Google account.

## Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings, under third-party connections.
